# Delivery roadmap

- **PR 1 – Foundation:** Cloudflare Worker + D1 schema + secure authentication, migrations, start-up instructions.
- **PR 2 – ERP shell:** Desk, navigation, Work Register, Client Master, Document Register, Team and Activity; accessible keyboard UI.
- **PR 3 – Workflows:** CRUD client/task/documents, assignment, audit events, status lifecycle, filters and CSV export.
- **PR 4 – Compliance:** configurable service templates, recurrence and deadlines, job idempotency and due date alerts.
- **PR 5 – Hardening:** test suite, role/permission matrix, backup/restore, privacy and import/export audits.
- **PR 6 – Integrations:** approved email and WhatsApp Business providers, consent and delivery receipts.
- **PR 7 – Pilot:** real-firm testing and fixes.

**Do not deploy to real CA firm data before the security checklist and pilot tests pass.**
